ROPE RUSH

A tug-of-war against the computer. Pull the rope to your side, but watch your stamina: every pull costs energy!

How to play
• Use NORMAL PULL (SPACE) or POWER PULL (SHIFT) to drag the rope to the LEFT.
• Pulling costs stamina. Stamina refills slowly when you stop pulling. Below 25 stamina your pulls are only half as strong.
• Reach the left edge to win. The computer wants the right edge.
• Match time: 60 seconds. If time runs out, the side with the rope closer to it wins.
• P or ESC = pause.
